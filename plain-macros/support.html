Common questions

How do I sign in? Enter your email. We send you a 6-digit code. There is no password.

A barcode is not found. Tap to make a custom food. The app saves the barcode, so the next scan finds it.

How do I delete my account? Go to Settings → Delete account. This deletes all of your data.
